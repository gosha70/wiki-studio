## Why

Two decision pages can record the same decision in different words, and
nothing in wiki-studio tells a curator so; the pages then drift apart.
`findings` reports a disagreement only when a curator has marked it with
`> [!conflict]` callouts. The issue 54 study measured signal (b), the Jaccard
similarity of `_tokenise(title + " " + the first paragraph of ## Decision)` at
0.25: 2 of 3 true pairs found, 1 false candidate, verdict "fail" on three
labelled pairs, which supports no estimate. Issue 121 asks for an
investigation first: re-run (b) on a larger corpus labelled independently with
the labels frozen first, settle how a decision page and its incident record are
labelled, and only then decide whether a model-free `findings` kind is worth
its false alarms.

This bundle does it in two runs. The evidence run (US1-US6) extracts about 215
decision pages verbatim from code-copilot-team's routing and pi specs and
memkernel's specs at pinned commits, labels them by question in a session with
no candidate code, has the owner review the labels, freezes the labels, the
two candidates (B0, the study's (b) exactly, and B1, B0 with a frozen text
normalisation) and the bar, and publishes the measurement whatever it shows,
as an evidence PR opened by hand. The build run (US7-US9) happens only if a
candidate clears the frozen bar and only after the owner has released 0.11.0:
it ships `possible-duplicate`, a warning in `findings` only, on by default,
silenced for a pair by a `> [!related]` body line (ADR-0006) and for a wiki by
`WIKI_STUDIO_DUPLICATE_CHECK=off`. No release step is part of either run.

Items, by precedence within one section: (a) if the section has H3 lines (`### `, outside fenced code), each H3 line with the lines up to the next H3 or the section's end is one item; (b) else, if it has bold paragraphs at column 0 (a line starting with `**` that is the section's first non-blank line or follows a blank line), each such paragraph with everything up to the next one or the section's end, its column-0 bullets included, is one item; (c) else each column-0 list line whose text after one marker (`- `, `* `, `+ `, or `\d+[.)]` and a space) starts with `**` is one item, with the lines after it up to the next column-0 non-blank line or the section's end (indented continuation lines and the blank lines between them). - **FR-005 (the title)**: The label text is the H3 line's text, or the text inside the item's first `**…**` span. From it are removed, once each and in this order: a leading identifier matching `^(?:ADR-\d+|D-[a-z0-9]+(?:-[a-z0-9]+)*|[A-Z]{1,3}-?\d+(?:\.\d+)*[a-z]?)(?:[.:)])?(?:\s+[—–-])?\s*` (covering `D5`, `D-upsert-key`, `S1.`, `A1`, `R2`, `ADR-1:`, `FR-012`); a trailing parenthetical `\s*\([^()]*\)\s*$` after a trailing `.` or `:` has been set aside; then one trailing `.` or `:`; then surrounding whitespace. When nothing is left, the title is the first sentence of the item's body after FR-006 (the shortest prefix of its first non-empty paragraph, whitespace collapsed, ending in `.`, `?` or `!` followed by whitespace or the end; the whole paragraph when there is none). The title is written to the frontmatter only.
- **FR-006 (the body rule and the page, pitch finding 15)**: `## Decision` is built from the item's text in four steps, the same for every form; no word is changed, added or reordered. (1) **The label is removed from the body**: the H3 line, or the opening bold span together with the whitespace and one separator character (`:`, `—`, `–` or `-`) directly after it. (2) **Each line outside fenced code loses its leading whitespace and then one leading list marker** (`- `, `* `, `+ `, or `\d+[.)]` and a space); blank lines stay where they were, so a list item and its continuation lines, and any nested items written without a blank line, become one prose paragraph, and a bold-paragraph item's bullets become prose. Lines inside a fenced block (opened by a line that starts with three backticks or three tildes after step 2) are copied unchanged. (3) **An `h3-adr` item's** `**Context**:`, `**Decision**:` and `**Consequences**:` lines (each also matched with the colon inside the bold, any case) lose only that label and the space after it; each label's line and the lines after it up to the next of the three labels go under `## Context`, `## Decision` or `## Consequences`, in the order the labels appear in the item; lines before the first label stay above the first section. (4) Headings, tables, code fences and blockquotes inside an item stay as written after step 2; the scorer skips them, as it would on a curated page. Inline cross-references (`(D6)`, `FR-012`) are words of the item and stay. **The page** `corpus/<repository>/<family>/<slug>.md` is the frontmatter `page_type: decision`, `slug: <slug>`, `title: '<title>'` (single-quoted, each `'` doubled, as `yaml_lite.unquote` reads it), `status: stable`, `last_reviewed: <the pin's commit date, git's %cs>`, and `sources:` with one entry `path: specs/<name>/<file>` and `sha: <the pin's full hash>`; then a blank line, then (for every form but `h3-adr`) `## Decision`, a blank line and the body, or (for `h3-adr`) the sections of step 3. The slug is `<cct|mk>-<spec name>-<plan|spec>-<nn>`, `nn` the item's 1-based ordinal among that file's items in document order, two digits; `<repository>` is `code-copilot-team` or `memkernel`. ### US3 — the labels, for the owner's review (S2, D3, D5, D9)

- **FR-013 (grouping by question and the six dispositions, D3, D5)**: Per family (routing, pi, memkernel, and any extension family), the labeller reads every page of the family and groups pages that answer **the same question**, naming each group by its question; a page may sit in more than one group. Every pair inside a group gets one disposition and a one-line reason naming the question; a pair in two groups is labelled once. **A within-family pair outside every group is `unrelated`.** True: `redundant` (the same decision in other words), `conflicting` (the same question, opposite choices), `supersedes` (a later decision replaces an earlier one, which a wiki would mark with `superseded_by`). False: `companion` (two pages about the same event or rule kept apart on purpose, such as a decision and the incident behind it), `distinct-by-precondition`, `unrelated`. The README states the labelling rule for a decision and its incident record (`companion`, false) with two examples named by path only: the issue 54 pair (`decisions/never-bypass-a-git-lock-with-an-alternate-index.md`, `incidents/git-safety-bypasses.md`, labelled `redundant` there, a label that stays frozen) and code-copilot-team's `infra-verification-as-gate` and `executable-artifacts-shipped-unexecuted`.
- **FR-014 (pairs across families and repositories, D9)**: `labels.json` carries a summary of all families with one line per group (its question) **and one line per page in no group** (its title). The labeller makes one pass over it and lists the cross-family and cross-repository pairs it finds, reading both pages before labelling each, with a disposition and a reason; an unlisted cross pair is `unrelated`. Every pair of the corpus is scored and counted (FR-018).
- **FR-015 (`labels.json`)**: Keys: `corpus_sha256` (of `corpus.json` at labelling), `families` (name, repository, page count), `groups` (`id`, `family`, `question`, `pages`), `pairs` (`pair` as two page paths in sorted order, `group`, `disposition`, `reason`), `summary` (`kind` `group` or `page`, `ref` the group id or page path, `line`), `cross_pairs` (`pair`, `scope` `cross-family` or `cross-repository`, `disposition`, `reason`), `defaults` (`within_family` and `cross` both `unrelated`), and `true_pairs` (the count of pairs labelled true, in `pairs` and `cross_pairs`). ### US4 — the freeze (S2, D4)

- **FR-018 (`protocol.json`: candidates, threshold, scope, bar, selection; D4, D9)**: Written in words in the README and as fields `candidates.py` reads rather than restates. **Pages and pairs**: every page of `corpus.json` (none is superseded); (2) every match of `[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9+.-]*://[^\s)>\]]*` is replaced by a space (bare URLs); then, after `_tokenise`, tokens are dropped that fully match (3) `[0-9]+`, (4) `(?=[0-9a-f]*[0-9])[0-9a-f]{7,40}`, (5) one of the template words `context`, `we`, `chose`, `over`, `reason` (the non-stop-words of `page-types.md`'s fixed template text, lines 130-131), or (6) `(?:d|s|a|r|us|fr-|nfr-|adr-)[0-9]+`. **The threshold** for both: Jaccard at least 0.25, computed exactly as `4 * |A ∩ B| >= |A ∪ B|` with `A ∪ B` non-empty (equivalent to the study's float comparison and free of rounding);
