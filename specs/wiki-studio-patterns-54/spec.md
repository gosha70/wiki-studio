## Why

A coding agent told to reuse existing code can reuse only what it can find,
and safely only what is current and scoped. Issue 54 asks whether a curated
wiki page can carry a reusable pattern (why it was chosen, where its
canonical implementation and tests are, what was rejected, when to look
again) with what wiki-studio already has, and says it is a research spike:
the deliverable is a recommendation (docs and a template, a separately
specified enhancement, or no change), not a schema, a page type or a
deduplication service.

This spike builds a study under `eval/patterns/`: memkernel #57's twelve real
decision records rewritten as `decision` pages plus two successors, beside
code-copilot-team's wiki as background; the lifecycle traced through the
real verbs with the test backend; retrieval measured with both rankers;
model-free duplicate signals compared with labels fixed in their own commit
before any signal runs; cited sources checked against a later commit; and a
report that answers the issue's six questions and ends in one of its three
outcomes. `docs/patterns.md` is written only if the report recommends a
template. Nothing under `src/wiki_studio/` changes, no release is cut,
memkernel and code-copilot-team are read and never edited, and no result
depends on a model.
